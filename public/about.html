About

Human behavior has always been at the center of my work.

My background brings together Human Factors, behavioral science, research, and years of applied experience working directly with people and complex systems.

Background

Human Factors meets applied human behavior.

I hold a Master of Science in Human Factors Psychology from the University of Idaho, where my work included experimental research, usability evaluation, cognitive modeling, ergonomics, and human-machine systems.

My professional career has also given me years of applied experience working directly with human behavior, assessment, training, multidisciplinary teams, and complex healthcare and social-service systems.

That combination has shaped how I approach problems. I am interested not only in whether a system works, but in how people understand it, interact with it, adapt to it, and sometimes struggle against it.

I am particularly interested in opportunities where research and Human Factors can make technology, processes, environments, and services easier, safer, and more effective for the people who use them.
